# Day 19 — 26 October 2026
## Laravel introduction and revised direction for Zonke

I participated in a Microsoft Teams session with Tobb Technologies’ Chief Technology Officer (CTO), Mr Bernard Sibanda, and the WIL facilitator. Mr Sibanda introduced Laravel and explained its relevance to the planned Zonke marketplace redevelopment. This helped me understand how the framework related to the project and to the UI/UX requirements prepared for the marketplace.

During the session, the CTO and facilitator explained that the project approach needed to change. Participants were reorganised into groups of three, and my group was assigned to Zonke’s UI and design work. I adapted my contribution to the revised group structure and project direction.
