# Day 20 — 30 October 2026
## Developing the Tobb Technologies Service Catalogue

I was tasked with proposing a practical way for Tobb Technologies to promote its services, reach more people, and attract new clients. I reviewed the company’s services and value to prospective customers, then developed a service-catalogue concept to present its offerings and encourage potential clients to discuss their IT requirements.

I prepared a proposal and a customer-facing visual asset designed for reuse in digital or printed materials. I grouped hardware, networking, and software services around customer needs, with the aim of helping prospective clients understand how Tobb Technologies could provide suitable IT support.

## Tools and technologies

- Service and customer research
- Visual design
- Markdown project documentation
